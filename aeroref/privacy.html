AeroRef Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 28, 2026

Overview

AeroRef is developed by Gateway Dynamic Software, LLC. This policy explains how AeroRef handles your information. The short version: we collect nothing.

Data We Collect

AeroRef does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no network connectivity of any kind.

Data Stored on Your Device

AeroRef saves your preferences (display theme, font size, sound and haptics settings) locally on your device using iOS standard storage. This data never leaves your device and is only used to restore your settings between sessions.

Third-Party Services

AeroRef does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs.

Children's Privacy

AeroRef does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
